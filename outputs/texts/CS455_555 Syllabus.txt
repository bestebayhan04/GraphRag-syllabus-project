Course  Content  
1
1.  Overview  of  Word  Embeddings  a.  Importance  of  semantic  queries  and  analogies  b.  How  to  compute  word  vectors  c.  How  to  use  pre-trained  models  d.  How  to  generate  your  own  vector  representations  e.  Visualising  word  vectors  
 2.  Introduction  to  Attention  and  Language  Models  a.  Mathematical  Foundations  of  Transformers  i.  Self-Attention,  Multi-Head  Attention,  Positional  Encodings  ii.  Tokenization  Strategies  
 3.  Natural  Language  Understanding  with  BERT  a.  The  Masked  Language  Modeling  Task  b.  Next  Sentence  Prediction  Task  c.  Fine  Tuning  BERT  for  speciﬁc  NLP  tasks  i.  BERT  for  sequence  classiﬁcation  ii.  BERT  for  token  classiﬁcation  iii.  BERT  for  QA  d.  Flavors  of  BERT  (DistilBERT,  ALBERT,  RoBERTa)  
 4.  Natural  Language  Generation  with  GPT  a.  The  masked  multi-head  attention  b.  Pre-training  GPT  c.  Few-shot  learning  d.  Fine-Tuning  with  Huggingface’s  Trainer  e.  Sharing  our  models  on  HuggingFace  f.  Case  study:  Classiﬁcation  problem  like  App  Review  Prediction
