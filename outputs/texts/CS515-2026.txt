CS 515 – Deep Learning
Spring 2026
Catalogue Data:
This course covers the theory and foundations of Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) and various
shallow neural network architectures, including single and multi-layer perceptrons, Hopfield
and Kohonen networks, and deep learning architectures such as convolutional neural networks,
recurrent networks, autoencoders, generative adversarial networks, and transformers. Students
are expected to develop deep learning systems for machine learning problems in computer vision
and/or natural language understanding.
Prerequisites:
CS412 or CS512 or EE566 or equivalent, or instructor approval.
Instructor:

References
•Jurafsky book (Online) – Very accessible for the basics of neural networks
•Ethem Alpaydin book (Online via IC) – General machine learning
•Haykin book (On SuCourse) – Signal processing perspective
•Neural Networks and Deep Learning– Long and detailed, limited scope but strong coverage
•Deep Learning (Goodfellow et al.) – Broad coverage, less readable
•Blogs and YouTube tutorials (shared during the course)
Grading
•Midterm Exam: 30% (Week 10)
•Home-works (individual projects): 30% (3–4 Python programming assignments)
•Group Project: 30% (Reading, implementing, and presenting SoTA models)
•Paper Presentations: 10% (present novel works/ideas without implementation )
•Bonus: Up to 10% (extra work, tools, submissions, contributions to vision library)
