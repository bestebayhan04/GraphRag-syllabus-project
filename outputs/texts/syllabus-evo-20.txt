Evaluation
30% Project Proposals
You will prepare twoproject proposals(each worth 15%) during the semester: one based on
a pre-set project topic and one on a free-choice topic.
Each proposal should include the problem definition, its significance, relevant background
information, and a detailed description of your proposed methodology (system design, algo-
rithmic flows, test setups, etc.) for studying and addressing the problem.Since this is not a
coding-oriented course, you are not expected to implement the system, run experiments, or
perform any results analysis.Each proposal should be submitted as a report of at most 6
pages, including all figures and tables, excluding references.
Pre-set project topics (details will be provided in a few weeks):
•Project 1: Implementing a Growing Neural Network (suggested after Lecture 6)
•Project 2: Swarm Behavior for Harvesting (suggested after Lecture 7)
These projects provide the research or engineering question but allow you are open-ended in
the methodology you may choose to follow.
For the free-choice project, you may select any topic relevant to the course that poses a
meaningful conceptual and technical challenge and is suitable as a scientific or engineering
question. It must be distinct from, and not too similar to, the pre-set project topics. (If in
doubt about your topic, consult me verbally or by email for approval.) This project will also
include a short presentation based on your proposal report.
Projects may be completed individually or in groups of 2. Because of the large class size,
group work is heavily encouraged; individual submissions will not receive a positive bias in
grading.
Deadlines for reports will be announced later.
70% Exams
There will be one midterm exam (30%) and one final exam (40%). Exams will be theoretical
in nature and biased towards (but not exclusively on) the evolution & biology content of the
course.
